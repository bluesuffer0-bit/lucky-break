# 🎲 Lucky Break - Voxel Sandbox & Fortune Crafting

**Lucky Break** is a dynamic 3D voxel sandbox game built for Android with Jetpack Compose and hardware-accelerated WebGL. In this unpredictable voxel universe, traditional mining is turned on its head: every block you break holds randomized mystery loot, crafting functions like a high-stakes slot machine, and legendary bosses await in alternate dimensions!

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 🎲 Unpredictable Random Block Drops
- Forget standard mining rules: digging dirt might yield laser carrots, breaking stone could trigger TNT traps or award flawless diamonds, and leaves might drop mystery eggs!
- **Mystery Codex**: Keep track of every peculiar discovery and block drop rate in your in-game Codex (tap the `CODEX` button).

### 🎰 The 9-Item Gamble Recipe
- Crafting in *Lucky Break* is an exhilarating roll of the dice.
- Combine 9 miscellaneous gathered items in your inventory crafting grid to pull the lever on the universal loot table!
- **Jackpot Outcomes**: Score mythic weapons and gear with powerful enchantments:
  - ⚡ **Thunder Shockwave**: Calls down lightning on targets.
  - 🪙 **Midas Touch**: Transmutes target voxels to gold.
  - 💥 **Explosive Instability**: Deals area-of-effect blast damage.
  - 🥬 **Consolation Prizes**: Or end up with a mysterious bundle of weeds and carrots!

### 🗺️ Quests & Alternate Dimensions
- **Gather Mirrorshards**: Mine cavern depths or gamble at cauldrons to recover fractured rainbow mirrorshards.
- **Defeat The Splitrex**: Hunt down a primordial multi-voxel monstrosity that divides into aggressive smaller parts when struck.
- **The Prism Portal**: Assemble 10 Mirrorshards to forge the gateway to The Prism dimension.
- **Harold the Flying Pig**: Ascend into the celestial realm of The Prism to confront Harold, the supreme airborne fortune boss!

### 🚗 Rollywagons & Exploration
- Hop into customizable voxel **Rollywagons** to zoom across the rolling terrain, scale mountains, and outrun aggressive nocturnal creeps.

## 🎮 Mobile Controls

- **Left Virtual Analog Joystick**: Drag in any direction to walk; push toward the outer rim to engage sprint.
- **Right Screen Look**: Smooth touch-drag camera rotation (yaw & pitch) with customizable sensitivity and optional inverted Y-axis.
- **⛏️ Mine / Attack Button**: Hold down to break blocks or strike enemies.
- **🧱 Place / Interact Button**: Place blocks from your hand, consume food, or interact with workbenches and cauldrons.
- **⬆️ Jump**: Climb blocks or leap over obstacles.
- **🛡️ Sneak**: Crouch to avoid falling off dangerous cliffs.
- **⚡ Sprint Toggle**: Lock into rapid movement.
- **1–9 Hotbar Selector**: Quick-tap hotbar strip at the bottom of the screen to equip tools, weapons, and blocks.
- **Top Status Bar**: Real-time display of current HP, hunger, and world XYZ coordinates, plus shortcuts for Inventory (`INV`), Quests (`QUESTS`), Codex (`CODEX`), Vehicle Driving (`RIDE`), and Item Dropping (`DROP`).
- **Tactile Haptic Feedback**: Feel every block strike, hit, explosion, and jackpot reward through subtle device vibrations.

## 📱 Installation & APK

1. Download the generated APK file:
   - **File**: `app-debug.apk` (or find it in the repository / GitHub Releases).
2. On your Android device, open the downloaded APK file.
3. If prompted, allow **"Install unknown apps"** in your device security settings.
4. Launch **Lucky Break** and begin your fortune-fueled voxel adventure!

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Platform**: Android 7.0+ (API Level 24 to 36)
- **UI Framework**: Jetpack Compose & Material Design 3
- **Rendering**: Hardware-accelerated WebGL 3D Voxel Engine running offline in an embedded Android WebView
- **Interoperability**: Two-way JavaScript Interface bridge connecting native Compose controls directly to the voxel engine
- **Language**: 100% Kotlin
- **Persistence**: Local storage & save world state
